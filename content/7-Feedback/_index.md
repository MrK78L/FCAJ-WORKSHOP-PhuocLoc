## Overall Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

The working environment is friendly and open. FCAJ members are patient and willing to provide support, including when questions arise outside regular working hours. The comfortable environment also helps participants concentrate on studying and completing their tasks.

### 2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team

The mentors and admin team are dedicated and supportive. They patiently explain unclear topics, answer questions, and guide interns when difficulties arise during study or project implementation.

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Major

The bootcamp is useful and relevant to my Software Engineering major while also introducing valuable areas that I had not previously studied. It helped me reinforce existing knowledge and broaden my understanding of cloud computing and AWS.

### 4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

During the internship, I gained new knowledge and experience, including deploying a web application through AWS services and learning basic cloud cost-management practices. In addition to technical development skills, I improved teamwork, communication, and my ability to face and handle problems. The mentors also shared practical experience that gave me clearer direction for my future development.

### 5. Culture and Team Spirit

The FCAJ culture is positive: members respect one another, work seriously, and maintain a friendly atmosphere. The mentors organize sharing sessions about new technologies, professional experience, and real situations. These activities help participants solve problems together and learn from one another.

### 6. Internship Program Benefits

The program provides not only an internship environment but also practical knowledge and experience. Participants have opportunities to meet mentors and professionals from AWS Vietnam and learn from their experience.

## Additional Questions

### What were you most satisfied with during the internship?

I was most satisfied with the opportunity to experience new areas of knowledge, meet new teammates, and learn directly from mentors and AWS community members.

### What should the program improve for future interns?

At this time, I do not have any specific recommendations for improvement.

### Would you recommend this internship to your friends? Why?

Yes. I would recommend that my friends experience this program because it can help them broaden their knowledge, become familiar with AWS, and connect with experienced mentors and community members.

## Suggestions and Expectations

### Do you have any suggestions for improving the internship experience?

I do not currently have additional suggestions because the program has already provided many new and valuable experiences.

### Would you like to continue participating in this program?

I would very much like to continue accompanying and participating in the AWS community in the future.

### Other Comments

I sincerely thank the FCAJ program, the mentors, and the admin team. They patiently helped interns resolve difficulties and generously shared practical experience and approaches to problem-solving.
