## [Blog 1 – Secure Data Queries with AD FS, Tableau, and AWS Lake Formation](3.1-Blog1/)

This article explains how enterprise users authenticate through Active Directory and AD FS, use Tableau to query data through Athena, and only view information authorized by Lake Formation. It covers SSO, temporary credentials, fine-grained access control, auditing, and query-cost optimization.

## [Blog 2 – Reliable Multi-Region Data Architecture with AWS Glue and Lake Formation](3.2-Blog2/)

This article presents a multi-Region Data Lake architecture designed for scalability, centralized governance, and disaster recovery. It covers S3 Cross-Region Replication, Glue Data Catalog, Glue Data Quality, LF-Tags, Athena, and optimization through columnar formats, partitioning, compression, and lifecycle policies.
